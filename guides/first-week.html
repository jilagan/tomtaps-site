Your first week with Tom Taps

A guide for parents and caregivers who are new to Tom Taps.

Get Tom Taps on the App Store · Free to start

The goal for week one

In week one, your child watches you use the board. Your child does not need to tap anything yet.

Learn the board

The 21 words at the top never move. Your child learns where each word is by habit, the way you learn a keyboard.

Below the core words are folders: people, things, toys and food. Open a folder to find the words for that topic.

Model while you talk

Modeling means pointing to the words on the board while you say them. Your child learns by watching you first.

  • Start at snack time. As you say “I want more,” tap I, WANT and MORE.
  • Go slowly. Tap one word at a time and pause between words. Tap the most important word twice.
  • Follow your child’s lead. Talk about what your child is already doing. If your child is playing with a toy car, tap WANT, GO and MORE as you play along.
  • Don’t test, don’t correct. Don’t ask your child to tap. If your child taps a word that is close to what they mean, accept it. Then tap the right word yourself.
  • Stay one step ahead. If your child uses no words yet, model one. If they use one, model two.

Plan the week

  • Pick two or three routines that happen every day, such as snack time, bath time and play time. Model in each one, every day.
  • Keep the board with your child at meals, during play and when you go out.
  • Practice STOP and NO when your child is calm. Then they are easier to find when your child is upset.

What to expect

By the end of the week, your child may still only be watching. Watching is how children learn at this stage, so keep modeling.

Recap

  1. Learn where the core words are.
  2. Model as you talk.
  3. Don’t test or correct.
  4. Model in two or three routines every day.
