Give every child a voice.

Tom Taps is a picture board for children who don't speak, or don't speak yet. Your child taps pictures to build a sentence, and Tom Taps says it out loud in English, Filipino or Spanish.

For iPhone, iPad and Mac. Free to start. Tom Taps Pro is $29.99, paid once. No subscription.

“I built Tom Taps for my son, Tom, so he could tell us what he needs, thinks and feels.”

Tom doesn't speak. Tom Taps started in 2014 as one father's project, made with Tom's speech-language pathologist. Now any family can use it.

What it looks like

Why families and therapists choose Tom Taps

Built around how children learn to communicate.

Words stay in place

Core words like I, want, more, help and stop never move. Your child learns where each word is by habit, the way you learn a keyboard.

English, Filipino and Spanish

Each board has its own language. Switch a board to Filipino or Spanish and every word keeps its spot. A bilingual family can keep one board in each language. Filipino is in beta.

Progress report PRO

A PDF you can send to your speech therapist or school: words used, new words, core words used and days of use.

Private coach PRO

Weekly suggestions based on how your child communicates. It runs on your device and sends nothing to us.

Fitzgerald Key colours

Word types use the colour code therapists already use.

Yours to keep

Boards are saved in the open OBF format, so you can take them to another app.

Works on day one

A full core vocabulary comes ready. No setup is needed before your child can start.

Model and build

A modelling mode lets you show words without changing your child's sentence.

Choose the voice

Pick a voice and set its speed and pitch. You can also record your own voice for the cards you make.

Guides

Short guides for parents, teachers and speech therapists.

Your first week with Tom Taps

Learn the core words, model words while you talk, and plan two or three daily routines.

What to model at snack, bath and play

Phrases for each routine, using the core words that never move.
