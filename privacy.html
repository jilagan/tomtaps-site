Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Tom Taps – AAC Board (the “app”) is a communication tool for children and non-speaking individuals. We designed it to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

We do not collect your data

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. You do not need to create an account to use Tom Taps.

Where your information lives

The vocabulary, boards, custom cards, photos, and recordings you create are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync (a Tom Taps Pro feature), that content is synced through your own private Apple iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.

AI Coach

The optional AI Coach analyses your child's communication activity to offer coaching suggestions. This analysis runs entirely on your device. No communication logs, suggestions, or usage data are sent to us or to any third party. You can turn the AI Coach off at any time, which clears its stored data.

Progress report

Tom Taps Pro can make a PDF report of your child's word use. The report is made on your device from data stored on your device. It goes only where you choose to send it.

Voices

English and Spanish words are spoken by the voices built into your iPhone or iPad. Filipino words play recordings included in the app. Nothing you type or tap is sent anywhere to be spoken.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information and requires no account, it is safe for use by and with children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Purchases

Purchases (Tom Taps Pro and vocabulary packs) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: support@tomtaps.com.
